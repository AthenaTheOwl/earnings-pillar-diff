# Corrections to MSFT 2025Q4

The memo under `earnings_diff/` is write-once (R-EPD-011), so corrections to it
are appended here, oldest first, each under its date. A correction leaves the
recorded action and revert threshold as they were. It is not the outcome entry.

## 2026-10-20: the watched figure is a lease commitment, and the delta mixed lease classes

**What the memo says.** `us-gaap:UnrecordedUnconditionalPurchaseObligationBalanceSheetAmount`
went USD 108,400M to USD 92,700M, -USD 15,700M (-14.5%), and the pillar effect
and revert threshold call that figure purchase obligations.

**What the filings say.**

- The fact sits on `us-gaap:LeaseContractualTermAxis` with Microsoft's own
  members, `msft:OperatingLeaseMember` and `msft:FinanceLeaseMember`. It tags
  the Leases note sentence on additional leases, primarily for datacenters,
  that had not yet commenced. It is a lease commitment, not a purchase
  obligation.
- [FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000095017024087843/msft-20240630.htm)
  (accession 0000950170-24-087843): operating leases USD 8.6B
  (`msft:OperatingLeaseMember`) and finance leases USD 108.4B
  (`msft:FinanceLeaseMember`). The memo's prior of USD 108,400M is the finance
  class alone.
- [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000095017025100235/msft-20250630.htm)
  (accession 0000950170-25-100235): one combined figure, USD 92.7B, for all
  additional leases, tagged `msft:OperatingLeaseMember`.
- Like for like, all lease classes on both sides: USD 117,000M to USD 92,700M,
  -USD 24,300M (-20.8%). Matching members one to one does not work here: the
  combined FY2025 figure sits under the operating member, so operating against
  operating would read USD 8,600M to USD 92,700M.
- The Purchase commitments line in the contractual obligations table of the
  same 10-Ks (MD&A, not XBRL-tagged) went USD 72,022M to USD 109,953M (+52.7%)
  over the same year.

**What stands.** Action HOLD and the revert threshold are left as recorded. The
threshold's "purchase obligations" is this lease figure, so the next filing is
read as the sum of its lease classes, whichever member carries it. The Q1
FY2026 10-Q (accession 0001193125-25-256321) tags its combined figure
`msft:FinanceLeaseMember`. Read that way, the pillar effect says capex rose
while leases not yet commenced stayed above USD 85,000M, which the FY2025
figure of USD 92,700M meets.

**What changed in the repo.** `epd extract` reads facts from a cached inline
XBRL filing with their contexts. `epd diff` sums lease-axis members per period.
`epd memo` and `epd show` name a purchase-obligation tag on a lease axis a lease
commitment and list the members summed. `reports/MSFT-2025Q4.jsonl` carries the
corrected row, with the first-reported values kept under `as_first_reported`.
Tests pin all of it to verbatim excerpts of the four filings in
